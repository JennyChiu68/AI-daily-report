- https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/gpt-6-1-sol-replaces-gpt-6-sol-after-just-7-days-with-near-astra-intelligence

- OpenRouter引用AA的未取整分数分别51.8 / 52.7 / 47.5，与表中取整一致。
